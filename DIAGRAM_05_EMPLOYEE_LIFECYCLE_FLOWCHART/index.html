    Employee Lifecycle Process Flowchart (BPMN Swimlane)

    Lion International Bank Human Resource & Integrated Payroll Management System (anbesa_hr)

    Aligned with Ethiopian Labour Proclamation No. 1156/2019 & Banking Governance

    1. Process Overview & Banking Realities

    In Lion International Bank S.C. (LIB), an employee's lifecycle is governed by strict commercial banking controls and Ethiopian Labour Proclamation No. 1156/2019.

    This revised workflow incorporates the verified operational realities of the bank: 1. Onboarding & Master Dossier: Bilingual data capture (English and Amharic script), 4-tier Ethiopian address hierarchy (Region \(\rightarrow\) Zone \(\rightarrow\) Woreda \(\rightarrow\) Kebele), and assignment to one of three employee classes (Managerial, Clerical / Professional, Non-Clerical). 2. Probation & Fidelity Guarantee Gate: 45-day statutory probation. Confirmation to Permanent status strictly requires verified Personal Guarantors and signed liability undertaking letters recorded in employee_guarantor. 3. Career Progression & Operational Movements: Inter-branch lateral transfers, acting appointments with allowances, and performance-driven promotions. 4. Separation & Final Settlement (Labour Proclamation 1156/2019): * The 4-Point Clearance Checklist: Pragmatic checklist verified by the HR Termination Officer (Branch cash/keys, IT accounts, Finance loans, Property ID badge) without over-extending system logins. * Solving the 2-Week Leave Archive Pain Point: Replaces the manual paper archive and broken Excel spreadsheet reconciliation with instant 1-second Leave Ledger calculation (Positive balance \(\rightarrow\) Taxable Earning; Negative balance \(\rightarrow\) Wage Recovery Deduction). * Statutory Severance Pay Automation: Enforces Proclamation 1156/2019 rules (\(< 5\) years service = 0 ETB severance; \(\ge 5\) years service = statutory formula). * Automated Certificate of Service & Guarantor Release: One-click legal letter generation.

    3. Key Operational Innovations in this Flowchart

    1. 45-Day Statutory Probation & Guarantor Gate: Matches Ethiopian banking reality—permanent confirmation occurs only after guarantor verification.
    2. Pragmatic 4-Point Clearance Checklist: Avoids system over-extension. The HR Officer checks off Branch, IT, Finance, and Property clearance and attaches the single signed physical form.
    3. Instant Leave Reconciliation (Solving the 2-Week Archive Crisis):
    4. Eliminates the week-long archive search.
    5. Leave Ledger Entry outputs the verified balance in 1 millisecond.
    6. Reconciles positive days into Birr encashment earnings, and negative overdrawn days into payroll deductions.
    7. Labour Proclamation No. 1156/2019 Severance Enforcement:
    8. Automatically assigns 0 ETB severance for voluntary resignations with \(< 5\) years service.
    9. Calculates statutory severance for \(\ge 5\) years service.
    10. Automated Statutory Legal Documents: One-click generation of the Certificate of Service and Guarantor Discharge Letter.
