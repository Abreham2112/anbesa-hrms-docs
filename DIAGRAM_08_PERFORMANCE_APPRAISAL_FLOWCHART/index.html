    1. Executive Modernization Rationale

    In Lion International Bank S.C. (LIB), performance evaluation governs staff motivation, annual bonus distribution, and internal career promotions.

    This updated specification incorporates the bank's exact operational structure: 1. Three Distinct Employee Categories & Appraisal Cycles: * Managerial: Evaluated Once a year (June 1 – Nov 30); evaluated on 15 criteria (11 base + 4 managerial leadership questions). * Clerical / Professional (Permanent staff > 45 days): Evaluated Twice a year (Cycle 1: June 1 – Nov 30, Cycle 2: Dec 1 – May 31); evaluated on 11 standard criteria. * Non-Clerical (Drivers, Janitors, Security, Messengers): Evaluated Twice a year (Cycle 1: June 1 – Nov 30, Cycle 2: Dec 1 – May 31); evaluated on 14 specific criteria 100% rendered in Amharic (Ge'ez script). 2. Standard 1 to 5 Rating Scale: * \(1 = \text{Poor}\), \(2 = \text{Satisfactory/Fair}\), \(3 = \text{Good}\), \(4 = \text{Very Good}\), \(5 = \text{Excellent}\). 3. Discrete Banking Score Conversion Bands (Average Score out of 5): * Average \(\ge 4.0\): Scored as \(100\%\) (Full performance rating / maximum bonus tier). * Average \(3.5 – 3.9\): Scored as \(75\%\) (High performance tier). * Average \(3.0 – 3.49\): Scored as \(50\%\) (Satisfactory / baseline tier). * Average \(< 3.0\): No Value (\(0\%\)) (Unsatisfactory; no bonus; ineligible for vacancies). 4. Verified Bank Sign-Off Hierarchy: * No Self-Evaluation: Form format prepared and dispatched by the HR Performance Officer. * Evaluated by the Direct Supervisor / Assistant Branch Manager. * Countersigned by the Main Branch Manager / Unit Head. * Mandatory Employee Review: The employee views their evaluation and digitally signs it. 5. Direct Banking Outcomes: * Yearly Performance Bonus: Distributed directly based on the percentage score tier. * Internal Vacancy Competition: Serves as the primary competitive score for internal vacancies, enabling career advancement across classes (even enabling qualified Non-Clerical staff to compete for Clerical and Managerial posts).

    3. Summary of Improvements in anbesa_hr

    1. Role-Specific Question Catalogs:
    2. Managerial: 15 questions (covers branch deposit targets, leadership, loan recovery).
    3. Clerical: 11 questions (operational speed, accuracy, customer service).
    4. Non-Clerical: 14 questions natively in Amharic (care of vehicle/property, punctuality, discipline).
    5. Automated Mathematical Banding:
    6. Eliminates Excel calculation errors; immediately maps 1–5 averages to \(100\%, 75\%, 50\%, \text{or } 0\%\).
    7. Double Supervisory Sign-off & Employee Acknowledgment:
    8. Assistant Branch Manager grades → Main Branch Manager countersigns → Employee reviews and signs.
    9. Direct Institutional Linkages:
    10. Annual Performance Bonus: Feeds directly into payroll calculations.
    11. Internal Mobility: Empowers internal promotion competition, including upward career progression for non-clerical personnel.
