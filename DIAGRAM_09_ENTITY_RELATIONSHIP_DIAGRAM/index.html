    Entity-Relationship (ER) Diagram Specification

    Lion International Bank Human Resource & Integrated Payroll Management System (anbesa_hr)

    Relational Data Model for MariaDB / Frappe Framework

    1. Conceptual & Logical Data Model Overview

    The Entity-Relationship (ER) Diagram formalizes the structural data foundation of LIBHRMS (anbesa_hr). It maps real-world commercial banking entities, attributes, primary keys (PK), foreign keys (FK), and cardinalities across 5 integrated functional domains:

    1. Core HR & Organizational Hierarchy:
    2. Reuses the legacy Employee ID (EmplID) for continuous audit provenance.
    3. Models the Department / Unit Tree (BusUnits) using Frappe's hierarchical NestedSet pattern.
    4. Supports M:N Guarantor relationships (one employee can have multiple guarantors, and a bank employee can act as a guarantor for multiple colleagues).
    5. Captures the 4-Tier Ethiopian Administrative Address (Region \(\rightarrow\) Zone \(\rightarrow\) Woreda \(\rightarrow\) Kebele).
    6. Leave Management & Ledger System:
    7. Implements the Immutable Leave Ledger (Leave_Ledger_Entry), permanently solving the Termination Officer's 2-week paper audit crisis.
    8. Reliever assignment and dual-calendar date tracking (Gregorian and Ethiopian Calendar timestamps).
    9. Payroll & Compensation Engine:
    10. Grid matrix linking Bank Pay Grade and Scale Step to Base Ethiopian Birr amounts.
    11. Detailed Salary_Slip capturing progressive Ethiopian income tax (ERCA), 7% employee pension, 11% bank pension liability, staff loan amortization, and leave reconciliation adjustments.
    12. Performance Appraisal Subsystem:
    13. Multi-template model supporting Managerial (15 questions), Clerical (11 questions), and Non-Clerical (14 Amharic questions).
    14. Itemized scoring table (scale 1 to 5) feeding into the converted discrete percentage bands (\(100\%, 75\%, 50\%, 0\%\)).
    15. Employee Separation & Exit Clearance (Proc. 1156/2019):
    16. Single-point 4-station Clearance Checklist (Branch, IT, Finance, Property).
    17. Automated settlement engine integrating severance rules (\(< 5\) yrs vs. \(\ge 5\) yrs) and reconciled leave encashment/recovery.

    3. Key Relational Design Principles in this Schema

    1. Provable Audit Lineage via Leave_Ledger_Entry:
    2. Instead of storing a vulnerable editable integer like remainLeaveDays, the system computes real-time leave balances using an immutable ledger.
    3. When an employee terminates, the system performs a query on SUM(days_credited) - SUM(days_debited) in milliseconds, permanently eliminating the 2-week archive crisis.
    4. Dynamic Performance Scoring Engine:
    5. Completely normalizes the questionnaire structure: Appraisal_Template \(\rightarrow\) Appraisal_Criteria \(\rightarrow\) Appraisal_Score_Detail.
    6. Directly supports Lion International Bank’s exact rule: 11 questions for Clerical, 14 in Amharic for Non-Clerical, and 15 for Managerial, with automatic mapping to the \(100\%, 75\%, 50\%, 0\%\) bands.
    7. M:N Guarantor Relationship (EMPLOYEE_GUARANTOR):
    8. Features a dual-link structure: employee_id (the guaranteed staff member) and guarantor_employee_id (if the guarantor is also a bank employee). This satisfies the bank's requirement that an employee can guarantee multiple colleagues.
    9. Statutory Labour Proclamation 1156/2019 Enforcement:
    10. The EMPLOYEE_SEPARATION table directly captures tenure in years, automating the 0 ETB severance condition for voluntary resignation with \(< 5\) years service, and linking to the 4-point CLEARANCE_CHECKLIST.
