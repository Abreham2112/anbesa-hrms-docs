    Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) & Maker-Checker Governance Matrix

    Lion International Bank Human Resource & Integrated Payroll Management System (anbesa_hr)

    1. Executive Summary & Banking Governance Principles

    In commercial banking, access control is governed by strict compliance mandates from the National Bank of Ethiopia (NBE), external auditors, and internal risk management policies.

    The security architecture of LIBHRMS (anbesa_hr) is built on three core pillars: 1. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Privileges are assigned to roles, never individual users. All bank users inherit the base Employee role, while administrative privileges are additive. 2. Segregation of Duties (SoD) & Maker-Checker Principle: High-risk transactions (salary scale adjustments, promotions, disciplinary terminations, and monthly payroll disbursement) cannot be executed by a single individual. The person who drafts a transaction (The Maker) can never be the person who finalizes and authorizes it (The Checker). 3. Branch-Level Data Isolation (Row-Level Security): Branch Managers and local supervisors are restricted via Frappe User Permissions to view and act upon only employees assigned to their specific Branch / Department. Head Office HR and Finance maintain bank-wide visibility.

    3. Bank System Roles Catalog

    Role Name Scope of Visibility Core Responsibilities in System
    1. Employee (Base ESS) Self-Only (owner = user) Universal base role. View payslips, submit leave, self-appraisal, update bio-data.
    2. Branch / Line Manager (MSS) Direct Branch / Team Only Approves leave, conducts performance evaluations, endorses transfer/acting requests.
    3. HR Operations Officer (Maker) Bank-wide (Head Office & Branches) Prepares employee master dossiers, guarantor forms, transfers, and training history.
    4. HR Director / Manager (Checker) Bank-wide Authorizes employee promotions, salary scale steps, disciplinary actions, and exit clearance.
    5. Payroll Officer (Maker) Bank-wide Configures salary components, processes monthly payroll batches, reconciles tax/pension.
    6. Finance Director / Checker Bank-wide Verifies payroll calculations, authorizes final salary run, signs off on CBS Batch Export File.
    7. System Administrator Technical System Configuration Manages user accounts, assigns roles, configures Ethiopian calendar and system backups.
    8. Internal Auditor Bank-wide (Strictly Read-Only) Comprehensive view-only access across all HR dossiers, payroll runs, and audit logs.

    4. Comprehensive RBAC Permission Matrix

    Permission Legend:

    • C: Create (Draft new record)
    • R: Read (View records)
    • U: Update (Edit draft records)
    • D: Delete (Purge records — strictly locked down in banking)
    • S: Submit (Freeze record into immutable posted state)
    • X: Cancel / Amend (Void a submitted record with an audit reason)
    • E: Export (Download to Excel / CSV / PDF)
    • [Own]: Restricted strictly to records where the employee is the record owner.
    • [Branch]: Restricted strictly to employees within the manager's assigned branch.

    5. High-Risk Banking Workflows: Maker-Checker Governance

    High-Risk Transaction The Maker (Initiator) The Checker (Authorizer) Mandatory Control / Safeguard
    1. Monthly Payroll Disbursement Payroll Officer
    Prepares calculations, verifies deductions, checks attendance/unpaid leave.    		 Finance Director
    Reconciles bank-wide totals against general ledger, signs off on and exports the CBS Batch File.    		 The Payroll Officer cannot export the final CBS file without the Finance Director's formal digital submission (docstatus = 1).
    2. Staff Promotion & Salary Increment HR Operations Officer
    Drafts promotion details based on approved appraisal ratings and vacant positions.    		 HR Director
    Verifies budget allocation, approves pay scale step, and officially commits the promotion.    		 Automated system rule prevents retroactive increments without Board/Executive committee reference number.
    3. Employee Resignation & Exit Clearance Department / Branch Manager
    Signs off branch property, office keys, and cashier cash register handover.    		 HR Director & Finance Officer
    Confirms loan settlement, releases fidelity guarantor liability, and approves final severance payout.    		 Employee status cannot transition to "Terminated" while any clearance sign-off checkpoint remains pending.
    4. Fidelity Guarantor Registration HR Operations Officer
    Inputs guarantor personal details and scans collateral letters.    		 HR Director / Legal Unit
    Verifies physical guarantor undertaking letters against employer bank confirmation.    		 An employee on probation cannot be converted to Permanent without an approved Guarantor record.
    5. Unused Leave Birr Encashment HR Operations Officer
    Calculates monetary value using the standard daily wage formula.    		 Finance Officer
    Verifies tax calculation and includes the encashed amount in the monthly payroll batch.    		 Encashment requests are validated against the immutable Leave Ledger before being released to Payroll.
